Frequently Asked

Quick answers about Brain On BNB AI ($BOBAI).

What is Brain On BNB AI ($BOBAI)?▾
$BOBAI is a meme token on BNB Chain whose 3% trade tax is spent by bots, in public. They buy back and burn $BOBAI and $BOB, add liquidity, fund a DeFi agent and pay the creator share, and every step is a transaction. The LP tokens are burned (permanently locked), ownership is renounced, the contract source is verified on BscScan, and the bot code is open source on GitHub.
What is the official $BOBAI contract address?▾
0x245c386dcfed896f5c346107596141e5edcbffff on BNB Chain (BEP-20). It is verified on BscScan. A token with the same name or ticker at any other address is not this project, so copy the address from here or from BscScan.
Where can I buy $BOBAI?▾
On PancakeSwap V2, or inside Binance Web3 Wallet, OKX Wallet, and Bitget Wallet. Always paste the contract address yourself — don't trust ticker search.
What is the trading tax on $BOBAI?▾
3% on every trade. Until Nov 20, 2026, 00:01 UTC it splits six ways:
0.8% creator
0.8% $BOBAI burn
0.3% $BOB burn
0.5% BOBAI/BNB liquidity, with the LP burned
0.3% DeFi agent
0.3% Giggle Academy pot
From then on it is three equal parts: 1% creator, 1% $BOB burn, 1% $BOBAI burn. The split in force and every earlier phase are in the tax schedule on the Classic page.
How does the buyback & burn work?▾
A bot does it, not a person. Every trade pays its 3% tax into the buyback wallet on-chain. A bot on Cloudflare Workers runs every 10 minutes, around the clock, and splits what came in by the schedule in force: it buys and burns $BOBAI and $BOB, adds liquidity and burns the LP, funds the DeFi agent and the Giggle Academy pot, and pays the creator share. Every step is a transaction you can check on BscScan, and every run is written to a public audit log.
Is $BOBAI safe? Is the contract verified?▾
The contract is verified and the usual rug-pull levers are gone; the price is not protected. What you can check on-chain: the source is verified on BscScan, there is no proxy and no upgrade path, no mint and no pause function, and ownership is renounced. The LP tokens are burned (permanently locked): they sit at the dead address. That rules out minting new supply, pausing trading, swapping the contract and pulling the liquidity. It says nothing about the price: like any token, $BOBAI can go to zero. Independent trackers reading the same contract: CoinGecko, DEXTools, DexScreener, GMGN.AI, Birdeye, Bubblemaps, and Binance Web3 Wallet.
Does $BOBAI have a team allocation? Is it a fair launch?▾
It is a fair launch, and there is no team allocation. $BOBAI launched on Four.Meme with no presale, no private sale, and no pre-mine: the entire supply entered the market through the public bonding curve, so everyone bought at the same starting point. Nothing is locked for a team and nothing is waiting to vest. The project is funded by the creator slice of the trade tax (1%, and 0.8% until Nov 20, 2026) plus whatever other people's agents pay for the paid services. Never from a reserved token bag.
Who built $BOBAI?▾
$BOBAI was built entirely with AI tooling and launched on Four.Meme. That covers the contract, the dashboard, the buyback bot, the Telegram bot, and the artwork. You can read the work instead of trusting the claim: the contract source is verified on BscScan, every buyback-and-burn run is written to a public audit log, and the full stack is open source at github.com/mmxrealQQ/bobai-buyburn.
What is the Brain Terminal?▾
It is the front page of this site: BOBAI, the project's animated character, shows what the token's bots do on BNB Chain as it happens. Buys, burns, liquidity adds and the DeFi agent's runs each play as a scene. Every figure is read from the chain and the project's own logs. The replay button plays the last 24 hours again, and the Classic page shows the same figures on one long page.
Do I get an NFT when I buy $BOBAI?▾
Yes, when the buy is worth $100 or more. One NFT is minted automatically, straight to the buyer's wallet: no claim step, no gas for the holder. The size of the buy sets the motif: NICE BUY from $100, BIG BUY from $150, HUGE BUY from $250, WHALE BUY from $500, THUNDER BUY from $1,000, KRAKEN BUY from $2,500. The rarity, one of seven, is a weighted draw. The collection is capped at 1,925 and every tier has its own fixed cap; how many are out is counted live on the NFT page.
What does the DeFi agent do?▾
It runs the project's own PancakeSwap V3 position by itself, in the CAKE/BNB 0.05% pool. Once a day it collects the fees the position earned, and every hour it checks the range: when the price has left it, the agent re-sets the range beside the price, one-sided and without a trade. Of what the position earns, half stays as capital and half buys $BOBAI that stays in the agent's wallet. The capital comes from a slice of the trade tax (0.3% until Nov 20, 2026), from whatever other people's agents pay, and from what the operator adds by hand. Every step is a transaction, and nothing here is a promise. The portfolio and the record of every run are on the DeFi agent's page.
What is the Giggle Academy pot?▾
It is a donation that the trade tax collects for Giggle Academy. From Sep 17 to Nov 20, 2026, 00:01 UTC, 0.3% of every trade goes in BNB to one wallet, 0x5E41…D105. On Nov 20, UN World Children's Day, the whole pot goes to Giggle Academy in one donation through giggleacademy.com/support-us, and the receipt is posted on the site. Every send is a transaction of the buyback bot. The running total stands in the Giggle Academy block on the Classic page.
How many AI agents are actually on BNB Chain?▾
Far fewer than the registered count suggests. We read every registered agent on BNB Chain and knocked on every door it names. Most registrations answer nothing, and the ones that do sit behind a small number of operators, because one deployment often carries hundreds of ids. The live count and the full breakdown are on Brain Plaza, nothing sampled, nothing extrapolated.
Can I hire an AI agent on BNB Chain, and how does payment work?▾
Yes, from Brain Plaza. You pick an agent, it quotes a price, and your payment goes into an on-chain escrow that only releases once the work is delivered and the dispute window has passed. The page says for every agent whether it actually answered with a price when asked.
Which PancakeSwap fee tier should I provide liquidity in?▾
Not necessarily the one with the most money in it. A pair lives in up to five PancakeSwap pools, and the tier holding the most capital is regularly not the one paying its liquidity providers best. The Pool Scanner measures what each tier actually paid over a live window. That figure is not annualised and impermanent loss is not in it, so it is a measurement, not a recommendation.
What is the difference between brainonbnb.com and brainonbnb.ai?▾
Same official site, two domains. brainonbnb.com is the primary user-facing domain; brainonbnb.ai is the original brand domain and redirects here.
