Anonymous self-screening

Free self-tests for mental health, personality and IQ.

Thirteen tests, among them established questionnaires such as the PHQ-9 (depression), GAD-7 (anxiety) and ASRS (ADHD). Each takes between 2 and 30 minutes. No account, and your answers never leave your browser. You see the result at once and can print it or save it as a PDF. A screening gives an indication; it is not a diagnosis.

In focus

ADHD, IQ and character.

The ADHD screening combines two published scales (ASRS v1.1 and WURS-K). The IQ test and the character profile are our own instruments: they follow established models (CHC, Dark Tetrad), are not clinically validated and give an orientation only.

ADHD in adults

ADHD screening

Structured self-report based on the ASRS v1.1 (WHO) for current symptoms, combined with the WURS-K for the retrospective assessment of childhood symptoms — in line with the current standard of adult ADHD diagnostics under DSM-5-TR and ICD-11.

  • Items43 (18 + 25)
  • ResultsInstant, printable
  • Based onASRS v1.1, WURS-K

Cognitive performance

IQ test

Performance test with items from the four central domains of the CHC model: fluid intelligence (matrices), crystallised intelligence (verbal analogies), quantitative reasoning (number series) and visual-spatial reasoning. The result is an estimate on the standard IQ scale (M = 100, SD = 15) with a 95% confidence interval.

  • Durationapprox. 25–30 min
  • Items40
  • ResultsIQ score + profile
  • Based onCHC theory

Dark character traits

Character profile

An in-house instrument along the Dark Tetrad: the Narcissist, the Manipulator, the Psychopath and the Provocateur. Everyone carries something of all four traits — the test shows in colour which one stands out in you. Descriptive rather than evaluative: the labels are deliberately direct, complemented by strengths, behavioural examples and concrete tips.

  • Durationapprox. 6–8 min
  • Items28 (4 dimensions)
  • ResultsColour profile + tips
  • Based onDark Tetrad

Methodology

How the tests are built.

Items, scales and cutoffs are those of the published instruments and their current validations.

  • Clinical standards — DSM-5-TR (APA, 2022), ICD-11 (WHO, 2022) as the reference framework for all symptom thresholds
  • Treatment guidelines — NICE (CG142, NG116, NG222, CG31), USPSTF 2023/2024 and the German S3 guidelines for treatment recommendations
  • Licensing — all instruments used are freely usable (public domain, WHO licence, CC licence, author permission)
  • Language — the English wording of the published instruments; BFI-2 T-scores use the German normative sample (Danner et al. 2019)
  • IQ norming — standard score scale with 95% confidence interval, CHC theory
  • Data protection — everything is calculated in your browser (JavaScript); none of your answers are transmitted
Notes

Important for interpreting your result.

Three things to know before you rely on a result.

In an acute crisis

If you are currently under severe strain or in a mental health crisis, help is available around the clock — free of charge and confidential:

  • Your local emergency number – e.g. 112 (EU), 911 (US), 999 (UK) in acute danger
  • 988 – Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US & Canada, 24/7, call or text)
  • findahelpline.com – free, verified crisis helplines in 130+ countries
  • befrienders.org – worldwide emotional support network (Samaritans)

Not a substitute for a medical diagnosis

The instruments offered here are screening tools. They provide indications of clinically relevant symptoms or of your individual performance or personality profile, but they are no substitute for a specialist medical, psychological or psychiatric diagnosis. A confirmed diagnosis requires a structured clinical interview, a detailed history and, where appropriate, further psychometric testing.

Anonymity and data protection

All tests run entirely in your browser. Your answers are never sent to us or to anyone else: there is no account, no database and no logging of answers. So that you can pause a test or return to your result, the answers are kept in this browser tab only. They are gone when you close the tab, or at once with "Delete previous answers and restart" on the test page. Printing and PDF export happen on your device.

No warranty

All results are provided without warranty. If your result is concerning or your symptoms persist, please contact a specialist medical or psychotherapeutic practice.
