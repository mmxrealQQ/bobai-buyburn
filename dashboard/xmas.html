Build the Xmas terminal ❄

The $BOBAI Brain Terminal shows every trade, burn and liquidity add of $BOBAI on BNB Chain as it happens. This December it wears Xmas looks built by the community — anyone, people, teams, platforms. From 1 December visitors pick one by its builder's name, and the terminal counts how many use each.

npx https://brainonbnb.com/xmas/kit.tgz

Node 18 or newer. It asks two things — what your terminal is called and who the builder is — and makes your folder.

Then you build it the way we build ours

  1. Open the folder with your AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini…). It reads the guide in the folder and knows everything we know: the layers, every event the terminal sends, the rules, the commands.
  2. node xmas.mjs dev — the real terminal, live data, with your folder in it. Save, and it shows.
  3. Pictures and clips with your own keys and credits, the same way the terminal's own are made: node xmas.mjs image "…" (OpenAI) and node xmas.mjs video "…" (Google Veo).
  4. node xmas.mjs upload — you get a preview link anyone can open.

What you build — what stays

  • You build the surroundings and your own animations: a scene behind BOBAI, an overlay in front of him, things that answer a buy or a burn. Nothing is given, nothing is expected.
  • BOBAI himself, the terminal's numbers, log, buttons and windows stay as they are, and the standard terminal is always one click away.

Everyone can build one

A builder, a team, another memecoin, a platform, an exchange — your name, your logo, your style. The rules are short: no hate, no racism, no terror. NSFW within limits is fine (memes, crude jokes, suggestive — no explicit sex, nothing with minors). No scam: no wallet addresses, payment requests or price promises inside a theme.

Safe by construction

Every theme runs in a sandbox, served from a separate domain: no access to this site, its storage or anybody's wallet, no calls out, no clicks, no sound. A theme that slows a visitor's device down is put away for that visit. A theme with hate, racism or terror is stopped at once.
