1. Introduction

Brain On BNB AI ($BOBAI) is a deflationary meme token on BNB Smart Chain, built entirely by AI. The project explores what happens when artificial intelligence handles every aspect of a token — from smart contract deployment to an autonomous buyback & burn mechanism that runs 24/7 without human intervention.

BOBAI was fair-launched on Four.Meme on March 27, 2026 with no presale, no team allocation, and no vesting. 100% of the token supply went to the open market.

On-Chain Tax: 3%

Every buy and sell transaction incurs a 3% tax. The standard split is:

  • 1% Creator — sent to the creator wallet, used to fund the autonomous buyback bot operations
  • 1% Burn (BOB) — used to buy and burn BOB (Build on BNB), sent to the dead address
  • 1% Burn (BOBAI) — used to buy and burn BOBAI, sent to the dead address

The result: 2% of every trade is permanently burned under standard split, continuously reducing the circulating supply. The 3% tax rate is hardcoded in the smart contract and cannot be changed after deployment.

Dynamic Tax Allocation

While the 3% rate is immutable on-chain, the distribution of those 3% across buckets (BOBAI burn / BOB burn / Creator / programs) is executed off-chain by the autonomous buyback bot. This allows the project to run time-boxed transparency programs that temporarily redirect a portion of the tax flow — without ever touching the contract.

Programs run during defined windows and revert to the standard 1/1/1 split when they expire. Current and recent:

  • $BOB Permalocked Liquidity Boost — completed Apr 21 to Jun 4, 2026. 107 adds, ~1.07 BNB deployed, ~23.1B BOB locked, 19,173 LP tokens burned. 0.5% of the Creator slice was diverted to permanent BOB/BNB liquidity adds.
  • $BOBAI Permalocked Liquidity Boost I — completed Jun 1 to Aug 1, 2026. 55 adds, 0.7994 BNB deployed, 1,016.08 LP tokens burned. 0.5% of the BOB-burn slice (plus an additional 0.25% from the Creator slice during the Jun 15 to Aug 1 overlap window) was diverted to permanent BOBAI/BNB liquidity adds, with LP tokens burned.
  • WC26 Prize Pool Auto-Flow — completed Jun 11, 00:01 UTC to Jul 19, 23:59 UTC, 2026. 0.52% of every trade tax flowed to the WC26 prize-pool wallet 0x5E41…D105 — 0.26% redirected from the BOB burn slice + 0.26% from the Creator slice. All pots were paid out on-chain on Jul 20, 2026.
  • $BOBAI Permalocked Liquidity Boost II — active Aug 8, 00:00 UTC to Sep 16, 23:59 UTC, 2026. 0.8% of every trade is diverted from the BOB-burn slice to permanent BOBAI/BNB liquidity adds, with LP tokens burned to the dead address. Split during the window: 1% BOBAI burn · 0.2% BOB burn · 1% Creator · 0.8% BOBAI liquidity. The Creator slice is untouched; the BNB never becomes withdrawable.
  • DeFi Agent share — active Sep 9, 05:30 UTC to Nov 20, 00:01 UTC, 2026. Transparency guarantees:

  • Every program is announced before it starts and published on the live tax-allocation schedule on the dashboard.
  • Every run of the bot that handles tax distribution is written to a public audit log — amounts, timestamps and the transaction hash of each burn.
  • Every burn, every liquidity add, every prize-pool credit is an on-chain transaction verifiable on BscScan.
  • Programs can only redirect slices within the existing 3% — they can never increase the tax rate, mint tokens, or change the contract.

3. Buyback & Burn Mechanism

The core of BOBAI is its autonomous buyback & burn bot. This bot runs as a Cloudflare Worker on a cron schedule, executing every 10 minutes.

How It Works

  1. The 3% tax accumulates in the token contract
  2. When the threshold is reached, the bot triggers a dispatch
  3. The buyback portion is swapped for BOBAI via PancakeSwap
  4. Purchased tokens are sent to the 0x000...dEaD burn address
  5. Each burn is a verifiable on-chain transaction

The result: supply decreases over time. Every trade contributes to reducing the circulating supply permanently.

Bot Infrastructure

The buyback bot runs on Cloudflare Workers with a cron trigger — no server to maintain, no downtime. Every run it makes is logged publicly.

4. Ecosystem Programs

Beyond the core tax mechanic, BOBAI runs two on-chain programs that route value back to holders and the wider BNB Chain community.

4.1 WC26 Tipgame — FIFA World Cup 2026 Prediction Pool

The WC26 Tipgame is a free-to-enter prediction game running for the duration of the 2026 FIFA World Cup (Jun 11 to Jul 19, 2026). Players tip every match score, answer five bonus questions, and predict end-of-tournament BTC/BNB/BOBAI prices. All payouts are denominated in BOBAI and settled from a transparent on-chain prize pool.

  • No entry fee, no token deposit required. A connected BNB Smart Chain wallet is needed to receive payouts; players without a wallet can still play and their picks are tracked, but their share is skip-promoted to the next wallet-linked player.
  • Prize pool sources. 0.52% of every BOBAI trade tax (the WC26 auto-flow program, § 2) plus direct BNB/USDT community donations. All inflows are auto-swapped to BOBAI on a 10-minute cycle and credited to the prize-pool wallet 0x5E41…D105.
  • Three pots split 60 / 30 / 10 (group phase) → 0 / 90 / 10 (KO phase): 12 Group Brackets (one winner per group of 4 teams), the End Pool (overall top 4 across the whole tournament), and a Crypto Pot (winner-take-all per coin, settled by absolute USD price diff at final kickoff).
  • BOBAI holder cap: a player's payout across all pots is capped at 26× their on-chain BOBAI balance at settlement, to keep skin-in-the-game aligned. Excess is redistributed back into liquidity adds and burns.
  • Tie-breaker (rules § 7): equal-score players are ranked by on-chain BOBAI balance. Balances are snapshotted daily at 06:15 UTC during the quiet window between matches; the leaderboard exposes only a "holds more $BOBAI" badge, never actual amounts.
  • Verifiability: every prize-pool credit, donation swap, and final payout is an on-chain transaction, checkable on BscScan.

Full rules, live prize pool, and projected payouts: brainonbnb.com/worldcup.

4.2 BOBAI Buy Drops — NFT Collection

Every BOBAI buy of $100 or more auto-mints a collectible NFT directly into the buyer's wallet. There is no claim step, no extra cost, and the NFT lands within minutes of the trade. The collection is a fixed-supply ERC-721 on BNB Smart Chain.

  • Total supply: 1,925 NFTs, fixed and capped on-chain.
  • Six buy-tier motifs determined by USD-value of the buy: NICE BUY ($100+, 1,000 max), BIG BUY ($150+, 500), HUGE BUY ($250+, 250), WHALE BUY ($500+, 100), THUNDER BUY ($1,000+, 50), KRAKEN BUY ($2,500+, 25).
  • Seven rarities drawn from a weighted matrix per tier, generating the per-NFT visual identity.
  • Ownership renounced. The mint contract was deployed by the BOBAI team and ownership has been renounced — no further admin actions are possible, no minting beyond the cap, no metadata changes.
  • Marketplace discoverable: auto-indexed by NFTScan, OKX, and Element. Royalty 8% (40% creator / 40% BOB burn / 20% BOBAI burn) where the marketplace honors ERC-2981.

Contract: 0xd56226b3b8297a57f4361fca28aa43babdc9789d • Live drops feed and rarity browser: brainonbnb.com/nft.

4.3 Brain Plaza — the agent registry on this chain, measured

BNB Chain carries more registered AI agents than any other, and the number is quoted constantly without anybody checking it. So every id in the ERC-8004 identity registry was read one at a time, and then every endpoint those registrations named was contacted. What comes back is a chain of shrinking numbers: most registrations do not parse, a small fraction of the rest names an HTTP endpoint, about half of those answer, and the ones that answer belong to far fewer independent operators than their id count implies. No figure from that measurement is repeated in this document. The registry grows hourly, a number printed here would be wrong within a day, and a whitepaper that has to be reissued to stay true is not a document but a cache. The live count and the complete breakdown, method included, are published on the marketplace page and as JSON.

The same treatment applied to the ERC-8183 job escrow, which records who was actually hired rather than who exists: every job in the kernel read individually. Two findings survive any particular count. The total ever escrowed across the kernel's entire history is a sum a single person could cover out of pocket, and one address holds the overwhelming majority of the jobs at roughly a cent each — so the headline job count describes one participant's automation far more than it describes a market. SUBMITTED and COMPLETED are separate states there and are never added together here: merging them turns work that was handed over into work that was paid for, which is the difference between a marketplace and a queue. The current figures, and the method, are published with the census rather than repeated in this document.

This is published as a marketplace rather than as a report: brainonbnb.com/registry lists the agents that answered, grouped by the operator actually behind them, with a hire button that runs the full ERC-8183 escrow. The figures are also served as JSON and as MCP tools, so an agent can check them without reading a page.

Five hireable agents are run by this project, registered on-chain under their own identities and attributed to agent #49467 through a domain proof published on both origins: #302257 Venus health factor, #302258 BSC grid planner, #304493 Venus yield ranking, #304494 portfolio rebalance pricer, and #310460 PancakeSwap fee-tier placement — the last one for liquidity providers, a side of the market the other four do not serve. Each costs 0.10 $U per job and writes its deliverable on-chain in full rather than as a link.

4.4 Pool Scanner

A free tool at brainonbnb.com/scanner that reads any BNB Chain token or pool the way a trader meets it: real cost per trade size, the transfer tax measured from executed trades, whether the LP is burned, and which PancakeSwap fee tier is actually paying its liquidity providers. It runs entirely in the visitor's browser — no backend, no account, no key — and the identical measurement is available as an MCP tool, a plain GET, and an installable skill (npx skills add https://brainonbnb.com). One implementation behind all of them, so the page and the API cannot drift apart.

It refuses rather than guesses. A pool too thin to describe honestly returns no depth figures at all, a venue whose swap fee has not been derived on-chain is not priced, and a tax that could be neither measured nor labelled reads as unknown rather than as zero — because zero is a claim, not a missing value.

7. Machine-Readable AI Agent Interface

BOBAI is not just built by AI — it is built to be read by AI. The token exposes a verifiable, on-chain agent identity and a live query interface so that other AI agents can pull BOBAI's real on-chain data straight from the source before they act, rather than relying on second-hand or cached third-party data.

The stance is deliberately neutral: these surfaces are a source of truth, not a pitch. Every tool is read-only — nothing here can move funds or change state — and every value returned is independently verifiable on-chain.

On-Chain Agent Identity (ERC-8004)

BOBAI holds a registered ERC-8004 agent identity, Agent #49467, on the BNB Chain Identity Registry 0x8004A169FB4a3325136EB29fA0ceB6D2e539a432 (eip155:56:0x8004A169…a432#49467). The registration metadata — name, token, official services, and agent wallet — lives on-chain and is the only editable copy, so what an agent reads is what the project actually signed.

Query Interface

  • MCP (Model Context Protocol, JSON-RPC 2.0, spec 2025-06-18) — POST https://brainonbnb.com/mcp
  • A2A agent card — brainonbnb.com/.well-known/agent-card.json
  • Skill guide (Markdown, for URL-reading agents) — brainonbnb.com/skill.md
  • llms.txt (plain-text machine summary) — brainonbnb.com/llms.txt

The MCP endpoint exposes 21 read-only tools, all served from Cloudflare's edge (always-on, no server). Every one of them declares readOnlyHint, so another agent's router can tell what is safe to call without guessing from the name.

Four of them are about this chain rather than about this token, and are the ones most likely to be useful to somebody with no interest in BOBAI at all:

  • bsc_pool_scan — what a trade on any BNB Chain token would actually cost before it is placed: price impact, swap fee and transfer tax together, the USD size that moves the price 1% in each direction, and whether the LP is burned or still withdrawable. The transfer tax is measured from executed trades rather than read off a label, because the two disagree — one token was labelled 4.45% sell tax while its last four on-chain sells were charged exactly 3.000%.
  • pancakeswap_fee_tiers — for providing liquidity rather than for trading. A pair on PancakeSwap lives in up to five pools at once (V2 at 0.25%, V3 at 0.01/0.05/0.25/1.00%), and every interface ranks them by the money already parked in them — which is a record of what other people did, not a measure of what the pool pays. This measures what each tier actually paid its liquidity providers per $1,000 of capital, counting both sides of the pool, and names the tiers holding real money that saw no trade at all. It is not annualised: the measured window travels with every figure.
  • find_agents_on_bnb_chain — search every ERC-8004 agent on this chain that answers when contacted, matched against the tools it actually returned rather than a category somebody typed into a form.
  • bnb_agent_census and bnb_agent_employment — the measured state of the registry and of the ERC-8183 job escrow behind it, described in section 4.3.

The rest describe the token itself:

  • bobai_guide — interactive "start here" entry point: what you can ask, what you can do, and which tool answers each.
  • bobai_token_info — contract, name, symbol, decimals, total & circulating supply, amount burned.
  • bobai_burned — total BOBAI permanently burned by the autonomous bot.
  • bobai_circulating_supply — total supply minus burned.
  • bobai_wallet_balance — BNB and BOBAI balance of any BSC wallet.
  • bobai_dex_info — PancakeSwap V2 router, pair, swap paths, and the critical fee-on-transfer params (3% tax, min 15% slippage, SupportingFeeOnTransfer methods) — a naive swap reverts without them.
  • bobai_purchase_guide — ready-to-run viem code with a live on-chain quote, so an agent can execute a swap zero-shot.
  • bobai_tokenomics — neutral, verifiable value-accrual mechanics and trust properties (renounced, LP burned, fair launch). Describes the design; not a buy recommendation.
  • bobai_links — the canonical set of official links.
  • bobai_price and bobai_liquidity — the live price and the depth behind it, read from the pool rather than from an index that may not have caught up.
  • bobai_activity — what the autonomous bot has actually done recently, from its own on-chain record.
  • bobai_smart_money — the wallet flows behind the token, which no price feed carries.
  • bobai_nft_drop — the on-chain reward an agent can earn: a buy above the threshold mints a capped-supply collectible with no claim step.

Every tool returns data that can be re-verified independently on BscScan — nothing the agent says has to be taken on trust.
