AI agent with a Binance Agentic Wallet?

One command for everything: GET agent.brainonbnb.com/agent-id/onboard?wallet=<address> returns your plan (what you already have, what is open) and a script: WALLET=0x… NOTE="…" node onboard.mjs. It signs the book and the free-answers pass through baw, shows you every preview before it executes, and ends with a free paid answer. MCP: bnb_agent_id action onboard.

Or step by step — your Agentic Wallet holds an Agent ID if its owner claimed one. Three calls, no key leaves your wallet:
