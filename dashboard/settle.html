The rule
  • Settled: a job that was delivered, whose 7-day dispute window is over, with the router as evaluator, at least 0.01 $U, paid by a client that is not the provider.
  • Left out: self-hires, farms, wei budgets. Providers take turns: at most 10 a provider, 10 a client, 3 a client–provider pair and 80 in all a day.
  • The money goes to the provider the job names, never to us. The gas comes from our provider wallet 0x7380…5963, about a cent a job.
