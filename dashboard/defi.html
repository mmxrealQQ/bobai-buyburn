The DeFi agent

A slice of every trade’s tax does not sit in a wallet. An agent puts it to work in a PancakeSwap V3 pool on BNB Chain; of what that position earns, a third keeps working, a third buys $BOBAI the agent holds and a third is paid out to the operator. And half of whatever other people’s AIs pay this project goes to $BOBAI too: part bought and held off the market, part added to the $BOBAI pool for good with the LP burned, as soon as it is worth the gas (every payment and what it bought). Every step is a transaction on BNB Chain, and the record below is read from the agent itself. Running since Sep 3, 2026.

How it works

One PancakeSwap V3 range on BNB Chain, in the CAKE/BNB 0.05% pool — with a small reserve range below the price when BNB waits — run by a program that reads and decides once a day and checks the range every hour. Nothing here is a promise; every step is a transaction on the chain and a line in the record.

The money

  1. 1
    InWhatever other AIs pay this project — a few cents per pool watch or per delivered job — lands in the income wallet. Once a day it is split: half goes to the operator in the coin it was paid in; the other half goes to $BOBAI — half of it bought and kept in that wallet, never sold, and half added to the $BOBAI/WBNB pool with the LP tokens burned, whenever the gas of that is at most 5 % of it (otherwise all of it is kept). The DeFi wallet runs on a slice of the trading tax and what the operator puts in by hand.
  2. 2
    At workThe DeFi wallet holds its main range in the PancakeSwap V3 CAKE/BNB 0.05% pool, and beside it a reserve range when BNB arrives while the main range is all CAKE — not the $BOBAI pool: the position earns on other people’s trades. That pool is the operator’s decision (Sep 11, 2026); the agent does not move, it gets better where it is. The capital stays; it is never withdrawn.
  3. 3
    OutOf what the position earns, a third stays as capital so it grows out of its own fees, a third is paid out to the operator, and a third buys $BOBAI that stays in the agent’s own wallet, never sold — the operator’s rule since Sep 9, 2026.

The program, every day at 04:23 UTC

  1. 1
    SweepIncome split: half to the operator, half to $BOBAI — kept in the income wallet, and a share added to the $BOBAI pool for good (LP burned) when the gas is at most 5 % of it.
  2. 2
    CollectThe fees the position earned: a third kept, a third into $BOBAI it holds, a third paid out to the operator. Small amounts are left to grow.
  3. 3
    RebalanceChecked every hour. A position the price has left is re-set as a wide range centred on the price again, with one swap that balances the two tokens — wide, because in the width record the fees of narrow ranges did not pay for what the price took from them and their re-sets, and a wide range is left far less often. The card says where the position stands against simply holding.
  4. 4
    IncreaseBNB above the gas reserve goes into the position — a deposit within minutes, when the range is live. BNB that waits beside a range sitting above the price opens a reserve range below it instead, WBNB only, no trade: a buy ladder under the sell ladder, merged back into one when the price has gone through either.

The record, run by run and in the agent’s own words: /lp/agent. The code: one core, shared/lp-agent.js, run by the worker and by hand alike, in the published source. It never touches the buyback bot’s own code.

The agent, on your position

The same code that decides about our position every morning, pointed at yours: in range or not and how much room is left, what it is worth, what it is owed and whether collecting pays for its gas, whether a re-set is due and in which width, what your spare BNB would add. It reads and plans. It signs nothing on your position.

Ask it

Paste a PancakeSwap V3 position id, or the address of a wallet that holds exactly one position. The look is free: in range or not, room left, value, fees owed. The plan costs a tenth of a dollar.
