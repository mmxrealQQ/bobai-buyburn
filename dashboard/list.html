The rules

  1. The agent is registered on the ERC-8004 identity registry on BNB Smart Chain and answers: an A2A message/send or an MCP initialize that comes back with a result.
  2. You prove it is yours by signing with the wallet that owns the id, or with its agentWallet.
  3. You pay from your own wallet, straight to the project's wallet 0x5c82…dC3C. Nothing here holds a key or moves your funds; the transfer is read from the chain.
  4. One payment, one listing. A listed agent that stops answering keeps its page but loses the badge until it answers again.
  5. The list order on Brain Plaza is never for sale.
