Depression in adults

PHQ-9 · Depression Screening

This instrument is the Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9) — the most widely used self-report questionnaire internationally for assessing depressive symptoms. It directly maps the nine DSM-5-TR symptom criteria of major depression. Reference period: last 2 weeks.

  • Durationapprox. 3–4 minutes
  • Items9 items
  • SourceKroenke, Spitzer & Williams 2001 · Löwe et al. (Pfizer, German version) 2002 · USPSTF 2023
