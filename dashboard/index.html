Block 06 · Questions

Frequently Asked

Quick answers about Brain On BNB AI ($BOBAI).

What is Brain On BNB AI ($BOBAI)?▾
Brain On BNB AI ($BOBAI, also referenced as brainonbnb / brainonbnbai) is an AI-built deflationary meme token on BNB Chain. Every trade triggers a 3% tax that fuels an autonomous 24/7 buyback & burn cycle. LP is locked, ownership is renounced, and the contract source is verified and published on BscScan.
Where can I buy $BOBAI?▾
Directly on PancakeSwap V2, or through Binance Web3 Wallet, OKX Wallet, and Bitget Wallet. Always paste the contract address yourself — don't trust ticker search.
What is the official $BOBAI contract address?▾
The official BEP-20 contract is 0x245c386dcfed896f5c346107596141e5edcbffff on BNB Chain. It is verified on BscScan, ownership is renounced, and there is no mint function or admin pause.
How does the buyback & burn work?▾
Every trade pays a 3% tax that accumulates in the buyback wallet on-chain. A Cloudflare-Workers bot triggers every 10 minutes 24/7: it unwraps WBNB, buys $BOBAI on PancakeSwap, and sends it to the dead address. Every burn is visible in the live table above and verifiable on BscScan — no human intervention, no off-chain logic.
Is $BOBAI safe? Is the contract verified?▾
Yes — contract verified on BscScan, no proxy, no upgrade path, no admin keys, no mint, no pause function. LP tokens are sent to the dead address (perma-locked). $BOBAI is independently listed on CoinGecko, DEXTools, DexScreener, GMGN.AI, Birdeye, Bubblemaps, and Binance Web3 Wallet.
Does $BOBAI have a team allocation? Is it a fair launch?▾
It is a fair launch. $BOBAI launched on Four.Meme with no presale, no private sale, and no pre-mine — the entire supply entered the market through the public bonding curve, so everyone bought at the same starting point. There is no locked team allocation and no vesting cliff waiting to unlock. Ongoing project funding comes only from the transparent 1% creator slice of the trade tax, not from a reserved token bag.
What is the trading tax on $BOBAI?▾
The standard tax is 3% per trade, split into 1% creator, 1% $BOB burn, and 1% $BOBAI burn. Campaign phases (e.g. BOBAI Liquidity Boost or Worldcup '26 prize pool) can temporarily route a slice elsewhere — the current live split is always shown transparently on this dashboard.
What is the difference between brainonbnb.com and brainonbnb.ai?▾
Same official site, two domains. brainonbnb.com is the primary user-facing domain; brainonbnb.ai is the original brand domain. Both serve identical content from the same Cloudflare Pages deployment.
Who built $BOBAI?▾
$BOBAI was launched on Four.Meme and built entirely with AI tooling — including the contract, the dashboard, the buyback bot, the Telegram bot, and the artwork. The contract source is verified on BscScan and every buyback-and-burn run lands in a public audit log.
